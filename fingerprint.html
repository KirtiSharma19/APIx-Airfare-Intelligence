WHY IT MATTERS

One flight should not become multiple observations.

The same flight may appear on an airline website and multiple online travel aggregators. Fingerprinting helps recognize these repeated listings before aggregation.

01
Identify

Match route, airline, flight number, date and departure time.

02
Compare

Check whether observations represent the same underlying fare product.

03
Deduplicate

Avoid counting repeated listings as separate observations.

06
